About UK AQ

UK AQ brings together air quality data from monitoring networks across the UK, making it easier to explore current and historical air pollution in one place.

The site includes live monitoring data, interactive maps and charts, air quality indices, WHO guideline comparisons, and articles about air pollution and its effects on health.

UK AQ is an independent project created and maintained by Mike Hinford.
