PNG export: 6× resolution

The master chart retains the full-month October GMT time scale. The 31 daily charts and the average-day chart use the same 1040 × 270 artwork and plot margins, a full calendar-day 00:00–24:00 GMT scale, and the same 0–4 µg/m³ vertical scale as the master. Each daily line includes the previous day's 24:00 reading at the left-hand 00:00 boundary, then the source day's 01:00–24:00 hour-ending readings. This preserves the continuous line segment from midnight to 01:00 as well as the exact calendar-day geometry. PNGs are rendered directly from vector artwork at 6240 × 1620 pixels. Line-only exports retain the full canvas and plotting geometry on a genuine transparent background. In browsers with folder access, a bulk button saves all files to a chosen folder; otherwise the browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads.

DaVinci Resolve hybrid animation assets

Oct 1 expands over 60 transparent PNG frames at 30 fps (2 seconds). Frame 1 uses the exact Oct 1 position from the month timeline. Frame 60 maps 00:00 to the chart's left plotting edge and 24:00 to the month-chart position for 16 Oct 00:00, using a smooth ease-in/ease-out. The other daily overlays and the average use that same final 00:00–24:00 span.

SHUN Winter monthly average UVPM line assets

October 2025 to March 2026. UVPM is derived from exact-timestamp pairs as UV 370 nm minus Black Carbon 880 nm, then averaged by GMT hour ending. Missing pairs are excluded and finite negative differences are retained. The transparent lines keep the same enlarged animation geometry and 0–4 µg/m³ vertical scale as the Black Carbon animation so the Y-axis does not move between sequences.
